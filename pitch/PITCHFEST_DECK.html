2 · Problem and target customerCognisom · Eyentelligence, Inc. · 02

Choosing an immunotherapy candidate means predicting whether a patient's immune system will engage their tumour. Today's tools each answer half of that, so a failure teaches you nothing.

The gap, concretely

  • Binding predictors return a ranked peptide list with no mechanism. Nothing tells you whether a predicted epitope matters in a tumour that is hypoxic, immune-excluded, or already selecting for escape.
  • Mechanistic systems-biology models carry that context but are population-average, so they cannot be driven by one patient's variants and HLA type.
  • Neither answers why a candidate should work in a specific tumour. So a negative result is uninformative and iteration is slow and expensive.

Who has the problem

  • Immuno-oncology discovery and translational teams in biotech and pharma, selecting neoantigen and combination candidates
  • Academic cancer-immunology labs testing mechanistic hypotheses about the microenvironment
  • End user: the computational biologist or translational scientist deciding which candidates go into assays
  • Application area: preclinical immuno-oncology R&D — neoantigen selection and combination hypothesis generation
We are not proposing a therapeutic and not proposing a clinical tool. The product is software and simulation sold into discovery, which is why the market we compete in is biosimulation rather than oncology therapeutics.

Mechanistic, not predictive

Cells follow rules drawn from published measurements and the tumour–immune outcome emerges. So a result decomposes into named causes a scientist can inspect and dispute — the property a drug programme needs and a black-box score cannot give.

Provenance by construction

Library parameters carry their source (PMID) or are explicitly flagged ESTIMATE. We can say which of our numbers are measured and which are guesses, per parameter. Coverage is partial and openly tracked.

Deterministic decision layer

A multi-agent tumour board ranks treatments with traceable evidence, model cards and provenance, under an FDA clinical-decision-support framing. It is deterministic by design — no ML model, no LLM — because auditability is the point.

The Omniverse step is a file handoff, stated precisely: the engine writes a USD stage and Isaac Sim opens it. It is not a live bidirectional coupling.

Why the filtering matters

Two well-known breast-cancer genes, ESR1 and ERBB2, appear in the raw calls and do not survive somatic filtering on this sample. A pipeline that reported them would look more impressive and be wrong. Reporting two genes instead of four is the result.

Engineering the long runs need

  • Checkpoint and restart, verified bit-identical to an uninterrupted run across 17 observables including the RNG stream position
  • Scaling measured and re-measured per module, not asserted
HCC1395 is a public tumour–normal reference pair, which is the point: anyone can re-run this. No patient data of our own is used, and none appears in this deck.
5 · The one external test we have runCognisom · Eyentelligence, Inc. · 05

A modelled mechanism was tested against human tumour data the model never saw, and the prediction survived. It is a surviving negative prediction, not a confirmation — and we say so.

The test

  • Prediction. Our MHC-I mechanism encodes hypoxia destroying MHC-I protein through the unfolded protein response while HLA mRNA rises. If that is right, a hypoxia signature must not correlate negatively with HLA transcript abundance across human tumours.
  • Data. 592 TCGA colorectal tumours, cBioPortal, RNA-Seq V2 — the same study, assay and statistic the source paper used.
  • Result. With IFN-γ partialled out, no MHC-I gene is negatively correlated with hypoxia. The prediction survives.
  • What it is not. mRNA is not surface protein; bulk expression mixes tumour, stroma and immune cells; and a null is consistent with the model rather than proof of it.

Three traps found while running it

  • GAPDH was used as a negative control. It cannot be one — it is a glycolytic enzyme and a canonical HIF target — and it came out at rho +0.41 with the hypoxia score.
  • On raw RSEM every gene correlated positively, beta-actin included. Something sample-wide inflates everything, so each value became a within-sample log ratio.
  • Hypoxia and IFN-γ are themselves correlated (rho +0.33) and every gene in the panel is IFN-γ inducible. The partial correlation is what the result actually rests on.
  • Each of the three would have produced a confident wrong answer.
We report our nulls on the same footing. A separate pre-specified outcome test on a clinical cohort came back null at p = 0.55. It is on the record and has not been retired or re-specified. A platform whose negative results are visible is the only kind whose positive results mean anything.
This is the whole of our external validation. One mechanism, one cohort, one surviving negative prediction. Everything else in the system is internally consistent and externally untested — which is precisely the gap this programme would close.

7 · The ask: one pre-registered experimentCognisom · Eyentelligence, Inc. · 07

Synthesise the peptides we predict from a patient's own variants and HLA type, and measure whether they bind. The prediction is registered before the assay runs, so either outcome is informative.

The experiment

  • Input. The three peptides our pipeline predicts as strong binders on HCC1395's own typed HLA alleles, plus predicted non-binders from the same sample as negative controls.
  • GenScript capability. Peptide synthesis, then binding measurement against those alleles — Experimental Validation and Closed-Loop Learning.
  • Registered in advance. The peptide list and the predicted call for each are fixed before synthesis. No post-hoc selection is possible.

Why either outcome pays

  • If they bind: the patient-specific neoantigen path has experimental evidence for the first time, and the claim changes from in-silico to evidenced.
  • If they do not: the error localises to presentation scoring rather than to the simulation, which is a far more useful failure than a model that was never tested.
  • Either way: protein expression and functional validation would then let us replace estimated immune-engagement constants with measured ones — the closed loop that turns a mechanistic model into a calibrated one.
And a second ask, which we rate as highly as the credits. Cognisom is built AI-first, which is why one founder covers genomics, simulation, parameter sourcing and rendering. The consequence is that the engineering is ahead of the domain review: our open questions are now scientific, not technical — which immune mechanisms deserve priority, which estimated constants most need measuring, which predictions are worth pre-registering next. This programme offers introductions to scientific experts, and that is the input we most need. We are looking to add research-level immunology, tumour-biology and translational expertise to validate and steer direction.

9 · What we do not claimCognisom · Eyentelligence, Inc. · 09

A platform is only as credible as the list of things it refuses to say. This slide is the list, and we would rather hand it to you than have you find it.

Not validated

One mechanism has survived one external falsifiable test. That is not a validated platform, and we never describe it as one. Our clinical-cohort outcome test came back null and stays on the record.

Not clinical

Research use only. No clinical validation exists, and nothing here predicts a patient's response to therapy. The decision layer is framed as clinical decision support and is not a cleared device.

Not experimentally confirmed

No prediction this system makes has been tested at a bench. The neoantigen calls, the binding predictions and the simulated immune dynamics are all in-silico today.

Not fully sourced

Parameter provenance is partial. Unsourced constants are flagged ESTIMATE rather than hidden, and several load-bearing numbers — immune kill rates among them — are still estimates.

Not GPU-resident, not agentic

The agent-based engine runs on CPU; GPU paths are opt-in. The tumour board is deterministic with no ML model and no LLM. Both are accurate descriptions, not modesty.

No patent claim

We make none. Our contribution to the commons is the published Bio-USD schema under Apache 2.0, which is the part we would rather be judged on.

Every item above is recorded in the project's own engineering notes, which is why it can be stated this precisely. We would rather be the team that volunteers its falsification criteria than the one that gets caught lacking them.
